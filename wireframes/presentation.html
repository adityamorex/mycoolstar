Proposed Customer Journey & B2B Integration Flow

1. The Discovery (Homepage)
Premium brand introduction & B2B trust building
2. Brand Story (About Us)
Establishing manufacturing credibility
3. Browsing (Catalogue)
Real-time inventory pulled from SalesOn ERP
4. Conversion (Cart & Checkout)
B2B flow featuring 'Pay via Credit Limit' and GSTIN capture
5. Dealer Retention (Login / Portal)
B2B authentication for registered dealers
6. Dealer Dashboard (Post-Login)
Live outstanding balances, invoices, and dispatch tracking
7. Internal Operations (Employee Approval)
Staff review before pushing Sales Order to ERP
